# Costivra mobile product bar

The mobile app is part of Costivra, a product being built to become a billion-dollar company. Apply the root repository's product doctrine here as well: high-caliber, simple, visual, trustworthy, and designed for finance leaders, business owners, controllers, and operations leaders organizing and reviewing expenses.

- Make expense facts, evidence, amounts, periods, confidence, deadlines, and next actions understandable at a glance. Use plain language; do not assume the user knows technical or AI terms.
- Design each mobile screen as a purpose-built experience. Prioritize the one decision or action that matters on a small screen; do not squeeze a desktop dashboard into a phone.
- Use clean layouts, generous spacing, subtle neutral borders, and rounded/squircle surfaces consistent with the web product. Avoid loud gradients, decorative bento layouts, excessive pills, ornamental glows, and generic AI-SaaS dashboard patterns.
- Do not use the conventional three-star/sparkle icon, `Sparkles`, a magic wand, or a similar generic Lucide glyph to mean AI, generated, intelligence, premium, or magic. Use a literal domain icon, a text label, a status dot, or a custom approved Costivra visual.
- Before completing meaningful UI work, inspect the relevant phone and tablet breakpoints, including loading, empty, error, and success states. Fix cramped, templated, or visually unfinished results.
